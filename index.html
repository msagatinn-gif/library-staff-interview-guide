Dear Library Staff,

I am a doctoral candidate at the University of South Africa (UNISA), currently conducting research as part of my PhD study titled:

"Utilization of Open Educational Resources (OER) to Support Teaching and Learning for Distance Students at the Open University of Tanzania."

The purpose of this interview is to gather expert insights from academic and administrative leaders to better understand the awareness, use, attitudes, and challenges related to Open Educational Resources (OER) at the Open University of Tanzania (OUT).

Your participation is important as it will contribute to the development of a proposed institutional framework to enhance the effective use of OER in supporting distance education.
